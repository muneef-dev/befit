06 / Before you visit

Good
to know.

A few things to help you choose your gear and plan your next game.

Talk to your venue ↗
How do I buy a product?

Browse the collection and open a product to see its details. Contact the Colombo or Mawanella showroom to confirm price, stock and how to place your order. This website proposal does not process purchases.

Are the products and prices up to date?

The featured products are taken from Befit’s existing catalogue. No live stock or pricing feed is connected here, so please check current availability and prices with the showroom.

Do you supply retail and wholesale customers?

Befit’s website describes both retail and wholesale supply. Contact the showrooms with the products and quantities you need to discuss your enquiry.

Which sports can I play at Befit?

Befit’s official website lists futsal, cricket, badminton and gym facilities at the Mawanella academy. Other branches have futsal contacts. Ask your chosen branch about its current facilities and training options.

What are the rates and opening hours?

Please contact your chosen venue for current rates, opening hours and available sessions. The venue team can help you arrange a visit by phone.

What should I bring for my session?

Check footwear, equipment and arrival requirements with the team before your visit. Equipment availability and venue rules have not been confirmed for this proposal.

Can I arrange training or a regular session?

The Mawanella academy describes training for young athletes. Speak to Befit about current programmes, coaching and recurring sessions; contact the team directly to discuss arrangements.

How do I change or cancel a real booking?

Contact the venue where you arranged the booking. Ask about its change and cancellation terms before confirming; no policy has been assumed in this prototype.
